Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ape Log Tech ("we", "our") manages information in the mobile application Eusko Abendaren Ereserkia.

Our application is designed to be used without the need to register or create user accounts. We do not request or store personal data such as your name, email address or password.

1. Information We Collect

Advertising and Diagnostics (Google AdMob)

We use the Google AdMob advertising service to display ads within the application. For it to work, AdMob automatically collects certain technical information from your device:

  • Advertising identifiers: IDFA on iOS devices or AAID on Android devices.
  • Technical information: IP address, device model, operating system version and language.
  • Location: Approximate location data (city or region level).
  • App usage: Data about interaction with ads and overall application performance.

2. Use of the Information

The information collected by AdMob is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  1. Display advertisements (personalized or not, according to the preferences chosen on your device).
  2. Ensure the security of the service, detect possible fraud and limit ad exposure frequency.
  3. Analyze advertising performance to keep the application service completely free.

3. Sharing Data with Third Parties

We do not sell or commercialize any kind of information. The only technical data collected is processed directly by our third-party advertising provider:

  • Google LLC (Google AdMob): You can review Google's detailed privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Privacy and Advertising Control

Since we do not store user accounts or identifiable data on our servers, no procedures are required to delete profiles.

However, you can control and manage ad personalization at any time from the settings of your own mobile device:

  • On Android: Go to Settings > Google > Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • On iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to enable or disable the advertising tracking permission.
